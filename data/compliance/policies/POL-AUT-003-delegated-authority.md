# POL-AUT-003 — Delegated Authority and Approval Thresholds

## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Purpose
This policy sets the financial and decision authority delegated to each claims
role. No officer may approve a payment, settlement, denial or exception above
the limits set out below.

### 1.2 Binding effect
A decision made outside delegated authority is void. The claim must be re-made
by an appropriately authorised officer and the breach recorded per POL-CLM-001
clause 10.1.

---

## 2. Authority Levels

### 2.1 Level definitions
| Level | Role | Settlement authority (AUD) | Denial authority (AUD) | Ex-gratia authority (AUD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Claims Officer | 10,000 | 5,000 | 0 |
| L2 | Senior Claims Officer | 50,000 | 25,000 | 1,000 |
| L3 | Claims Team Leader | 150,000 | 100,000 | 5,000 |
| L4 | Claims Manager | 500,000 | 500,000 | 25,000 |
| L5 | Head of Claims Operations | 2,000,000 | 2,000,000 | 100,000 |
| L6 | Chief Claims Officer | Unlimited | Unlimited | 250,000 |

### 2.2 Authority is per claim
Limits apply to the **total incurred cost of the claim**, being paid amounts
plus outstanding reserve, not to individual payment transactions. Splitting a
settlement into multiple payments to stay within a limit is a breach of this
policy.

### 2.3 Denial authority is lower than settlement authority
Denial authority is deliberately set below settlement authority at each level.
A denial above an officer's denial limit must be escalated even where the
officer could have approved payment of the same amount. Denial authority is
measured against the claimed value.

---

## 3. Mandatory Dual Approval

### 3.1 Dual approval triggers
Two approvers, the second at Level 3 or above, are required where any of the
following applies:
- (a) total incurred cost exceeds **AUD 100,000**;
- (b) the claim is a declined claim with claimed value above **AUD 25,000**
  (per POL-CLM-001 clause 7.4);
- (c) an ex-gratia payment of any amount is made;
- (d) a rebuttable exclusion under POL-MTR-002 section 3 is being set aside;
- (e) a payment is made on a claim with a recorded fraud score of 60 or above
  (which also requires SIU clearance under POL-FRD-004 clause 3.3);
- (f) a late-notification exception under POL-CLM-001 clause 2.3 is applied.

### 3.2 Independence of approvers
The second approver must not be the handler who prepared the recommendation and
must not report directly to the first approver on the same claim.

### 3.3 Recording
Both approver identities, roles, levels and timestamps must be recorded against
the claim. A dual-approval claim with only one recorded approver is a control
failure and must be reported in the monthly control report.

---

## 4. Specific Authority Rules

### 4.1 Total loss declarations
A vehicle may only be declared a total loss by an officer at **Level 3 or
above**, regardless of value.

### 4.2 Ex-gratia payments
Ex-gratia payments — payments made where no policy liability exists — require
the approver to record a written rationale and the business reason code. The
cumulative ex-gratia spend of any officer may not exceed **AUD 50,000** in a
rolling 12-month period without L5 ratification.

### 4.3 Exclusion override
Setting aside a rebuttable exclusion requires **Level 3** approval and a file
note identifying the rebuttal condition satisfied. An absolute exclusion may
not be overridden at any level.

### 4.4 Litigated claims
Any claim where proceedings have been commenced requires **Level 4** approval
for any settlement, irrespective of value, and must have Legal sign-off.

### 4.5 Recovery write-off
Write-off of a recovery receivable requires authority at the level matching the
receivable amount per clause 2.1. Recoveries below AUD 2,000 may be written off
at L1 without further approval.

### 4.6 Reopened claims
Reopening a claim closed more than 12 months prior requires **Level 4**
approval (POL-CLM-001 clause 8.4).

### 4.7 Catastrophe surge authority
During a declared catastrophe event, L1 and L2 settlement authority is
temporarily increased by 100% for motor claims only, for the duration of the
declaration. Denial authority is **not** increased.

---

## 5. Payment Controls

### 5.1 Segregation of duties
The officer who approves a settlement may not also release the payment. Payment
release is performed by Claims Finance.

### 5.2 Payee verification
Payments to a bank account not previously verified against the claimant record
require callback verification before release, regardless of amount.

### 5.3 Third-party payee
Payment to a party other than the named insured requires an executed direction
to pay, and **Level 2** approval where the amount exceeds AUD 10,000.

### 5.4 Payment above AUD 250,000
Any single payment above **AUD 250,000** requires Treasury pre-notification one
business day before release.

---

## 6. Edge Cases

### 6.1 Authority at the time of decision
Authority is assessed at the officer's level **on the date the decision was
made**, not the date of review. A promotion after the decision does not cure a
breach.

### 6.2 Claims that grow past a limit
Where a claim was validly approved at a level and the incurred cost
subsequently rises above that level's limit, the claim must be re-approved at
the higher level before any further payment is released. Payments already made
are not retrospectively void.

### 6.3 Acting arrangements
An officer formally acting in a higher role holds that role's authority only
where the acting arrangement is recorded in the HR system for the period
covering the decision date.

### 6.4 Delegation in absence
Authority may not be informally delegated. An officer's authority does not pass
to their subordinate during leave.
