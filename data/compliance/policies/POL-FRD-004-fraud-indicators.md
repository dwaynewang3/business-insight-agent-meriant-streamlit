# POL-FRD-004 — Fraud Indicators and Referral Triggers

## 1. Purpose and Principles

### 1.1 Purpose
This policy defines the indicators that must be scored on every claim, the
score thresholds that trigger referral to the Special Investigation Unit (SIU),
and the handling rules for claims under investigation.

### 1.2 Presumption of good faith
An indicator is a signal, not a finding. A claim may not be denied on the basis
of indicators alone. Denial for fraud requires substantiation under clause 6.

### 1.3 Scoring obligation
Every claim must be scored at triage and re-scored on any material change. An
unscored claim may not be settled.

---

## 2. Indicator Catalogue

### 2.1 Timing indicators
| Code | Indicator | Points |
|---|---|---|
| T-01 | Incident within 30 days of policy inception | 15 |
| T-02 | Incident within 14 days of a cover upgrade or sum insured increase | 20 |
| T-03 | Incident within 30 days before policy expiry with no renewal intent | 10 |
| T-04 | Claim lodged within 48 hours of a premium default being cured | 15 |
| T-05 | Incident occurring between 00:00 and 05:00 with sole occupant | 5 |

### 2.2 Claimant history indicators
| Code | Indicator | Points |
|---|---|---|
| H-01 | Three or more claims by the same claimant in 24 months | 20 |
| H-02 | Prior claim withdrawn after an assessor was allocated | 25 |
| H-03 | Prior claim denied for misrepresentation | 30 |
| H-04 | Claimant associated with a known organised fraud network | 60 |
| H-05 | Same vehicle claimed on by two different policies in 12 months | 35 |

### 2.3 Circumstance indicators
| Code | Indicator | Points |
|---|---|---|
| C-01 | No independent witness and no police report where one would be expected | 10 |
| C-02 | Damage pattern inconsistent with the described mechanism of loss | 30 |
| C-03 | Vehicle not made available for inspection within 10 business days | 20 |
| C-04 | Claimant unusually familiar with policy wording or claims process | 5 |
| C-05 | Pressure for rapid settlement or threat to escalate at first contact | 10 |
| C-06 | Incident location inconsistent with claimant's stated journey | 15 |
| C-07 | Vehicle recovered burnt out after being reported stolen | 25 |
| C-08 | Repair quote from a non-accredited repairer exceeding market by 40%+ | 20 |

### 2.4 Documentation indicators
| Code | Indicator | Points |
|---|---|---|
| D-01 | Receipts or invoices with altered dates, amounts or fonts | 40 |
| D-02 | Supporting document issued by an entity with no trading history | 35 |
| D-03 | Identical wording across multiple unrelated claimant statements | 30 |
| D-04 | Photographs with inconsistent metadata or evidence of editing | 35 |
| D-05 | Refusal or repeated failure to provide a required document | 15 |

### 2.5 Third-party indicators
| Code | Indicator | Points |
|---|---|---|
| P-01 | Third party and claimant share an address, surname or bank account | 40 |
| P-02 | Same third party appearing across unrelated claims | 35 |
| P-03 | Third party accepts full liability immediately and without dispute | 10 |
| P-04 | Injury claimed with no corresponding vehicle damage | 25 |

## 3. Scoring and Thresholds

### 3.1 Score calculation
The fraud score is the sum of all triggered indicator points, capped at 100.

### 3.2 Action thresholds
| Score | Band | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 29 | Low | Proceed with normal handling |
| 30 – 59 | Elevated | Enhanced verification per clause 4; handler may proceed |
| 60 – 79 | High | **Mandatory SIU referral**; settlement blocked pending SIU clearance |
| 80 – 100 | Severe | Mandatory SIU referral; claim locked; Level 4 notification within 1 business day |

### 3.3 Settlement block
No claim scoring **60 or above** may be settled until the SIU has issued a
written clearance or the score has been formally reduced with a documented
rationale.

---

## 4. Enhanced Verification (Elevated Band)

### 4.1 Required steps
For claims scoring 30 to 59, the handler must complete all of:
- (a) verify claimant identity against two independent sources;
- (b) obtain and verify the police event number where applicable;
- (c) confirm the repairer or supplier is trading and accredited;
- (d) conduct a recorded interview with the claimant covering the mechanism of
  loss.

### 4.2 Timeframe
Enhanced verification must be completed within **10 business days** of the
score being assigned. Claims-handling timeframes are suspended for this
period under POL-SLA-006 clause 2.3 and the suspension must be recorded.

---

## 5. Mandatory Referral Triggers

### 5.1 Referral regardless of score
The following require immediate SIU referral even where the total score is
below 60:
- (a) indicator H-04 (organised network association);
- (b) indicator D-01 or D-04 (document or image alteration);
- (c) indicator P-01 (undisclosed relationship between parties);
- (d) any staff member, agent or broker is a party to the claim;
- (e) total claimed value exceeds **AUD 250,000** and any indicator is
  triggered;
- (f) the claim is the third claim on the same policy in a 12-month period.

### 5.2 Referral timeframe
Referral must be made within **1 business day** of the trigger being
identified.

### 5.3 Handler conduct during referral
The handler must not disclose to the claimant that a fraud referral has been
made, and must not make accusations. Routine communication continues under
POL-CLM-001 clause 6.2 without reference to the investigation.

---

## 6. Substantiation and Outcome

### 6.1 Standard of proof
Fraud is substantiated where, on the balance of probabilities, the claimant
knowingly made a false or misleading statement or submitted a false document
material to the claim.

### 6.2 SIU outcome codes
| Code | Outcome | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| SIU-C | Cleared | Claim resumes normal handling; SLA clock restarts |
| SIU-R | Reduced | Specific components excluded; remainder settled |
| SIU-D | Substantiated | Claim denied under POL-CLM-001 clause 7.2(e) |
| SIU-W | Withdrawn by claimant | Claim closed; indicator H-02 recorded for future |
| SIU-I | Inconclusive | Claim settled with rationale; indicators retained on file |

### 6.3 Denial authority for substantiated fraud
A denial on substantiated fraud grounds requires **Level 4** approval
regardless of claim value, and Legal review where the claimed value exceeds
AUD 100,000.

### 6.4 Reporting obligations
Substantiated fraud must be reported to the Insurance Fraud Bureau within 10
business days of the SIU-D determination.

---

## 7. Edge Cases

### 7.1 Indicators that do not compound
Indicators T-01 and T-02 are mutually exclusive. Where both technically apply,
score only T-02. Indicators C-01 and C-06 are likewise mutually exclusive;
where both apply, score only C-06.

### 7.2 Score reduction
A score may only be reduced by the SIU or by an officer at Level 3 or above,
with a file note explaining why the indicator no longer applies. Scores may not
be reduced to accelerate a settlement.

### 7.3 Low-value high-score claims
A claim scoring 60 or above must still be referred even where the claimed value
is below AUD 5,000 and would otherwise be fast-tracked. Fast-track handling is
suspended on referral.

### 7.4 Innocent co-insured
Where fraud is substantiated against one insured on a joint policy, an innocent
co-insured's interest may be settled at up to 50% of the assessed loss with
Level 4 approval.

### 7.5 Indicators on denied claims
Indicators remain on the claimant record for 5 years whether or not the claim
was denied, and are visible to future scoring.
