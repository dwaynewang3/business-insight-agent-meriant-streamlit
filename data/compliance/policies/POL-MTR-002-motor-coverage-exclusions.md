# POL-MTR-002 — Motor Vehicle Coverage and Exclusions

## 1. Product Tiers

### 1.1 Tiers offered
Meridian issues three motor products. The tier on the policy at the incident
date determines cover.

| Tier code | Product | Covers own damage | Covers third-party property | Covers theft | Covers fire |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| COMP | Comprehensive | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| TPFT | Third Party Fire & Theft | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| TPPD | Third Party Property Damage | No | Yes | No | No |

### 1.2 Own damage on non-comprehensive tiers
A claim for damage to the insured vehicle under a TPFT or TPPD policy must be
declined under clause 1.1 unless the damage arises from fire or theft and the
tier is TPFT, or the uninsured motorist extension in clause 1.3 applies.

### 1.3 Uninsured motorist extension
TPPD and TPFT policies include limited cover for damage to the insured vehicle
up to **AUD 5,000** where the insured was not at fault, the at-fault third party
is identified and uninsured, and the insured provides the third party's name
and vehicle registration. Without an identified at-fault third party this
extension does not apply.

---

## 2. Covered Events

### 2.1 Collision
Accidental collision with another vehicle, object, animal or pedestrian, where
the insured vehicle was being driven or was stationary.

### 2.2 Theft and attempted theft
Theft of the vehicle or its parts, and damage caused during an attempted theft.

### 2.3 Fire
Accidental fire, lightning and explosion.

### 2.4 Storm, hail and flood
Damage from storm, hail, and flood, subject to clause 3.9.

### 2.5 Malicious damage
Intentional damage by a third party, subject to a police event number being
provided per POL-DOC-005 clause 4.3.

### 2.6 Towing and recovery
Reasonable towing and recovery costs following a covered event, capped at
**AUD 1,500** per claim, or AUD 3,000 where the vehicle is recovered from a
location more than 100 km from the nearest Meridian-approved repairer.

## 3. Exclusions

### 3.1 Classification of exclusions
Exclusions are classified as **absolute** or **rebuttable**.
- An **absolute** exclusion requires denial with no discretion.
- A **rebuttable** exclusion requires denial unless the claimant discharges the
  stated rebuttal condition, approved at Level 3 or above.

### 3.2 Intoxication — ABSOLUTE
No cover where the driver's blood alcohol concentration at the time of the
incident exceeded **0.05 g/100mL**, or exceeded 0.00 where the driver held a
learner, provisional or probationary licence. Confirmed by police breath or
blood analysis.

### 3.3 Drug impairment — ABSOLUTE
No cover where the driver returned a positive roadside or laboratory test for a
prescribed illicit substance. A positive result for a lawfully prescribed
medication taken in accordance with directions is not an exclusion under this
clause.

### 3.4 Unlicensed driving — REBUTTABLE
No cover where the driver was unlicensed, disqualified, or driving outside
licence conditions at the time of the incident.
**Rebuttal condition**: cover may be restored where the licence had expired by
**30 days or less**, the driver was otherwise eligible to hold the licence, and
the licence was renewed within 30 days of the incident. This rebuttal is not
available where the licence was suspended or cancelled rather than expired.

### 3.5 Unlisted driver — REBUTTABLE
Where the policy names specific drivers, no cover applies if the vehicle was
driven by a person not listed.
**Rebuttal condition**: cover may be restored on payment of an additional
excess of **AUD 2,000** where the driver was aged 25 or over, held a full
licence for 2 or more years, and had the insured's permission.

### 3.6 Young or inexperienced driver excess
Not an exclusion but a loading. Where the driver at the incident date was under
**25 years of age**, or held a full licence for less than 2 years, an
additional excess of **AUD 1,200** applies per POL-STL-007 clause 3.4.

### 3.7 Commercial and hire use — ABSOLUTE
No cover where the vehicle was being used for a purpose inconsistent with the
use declared on the policy, including:
- (a) carriage of passengers for hire or reward (rideshare, taxi) under a
  policy rated for private use;
- (b) carriage of goods for hire or reward under a policy rated for private use;
- (c) hire or rental of the vehicle to a third party.
Where the policy carries the rideshare endorsement RS-01, paragraph (a) does
not apply.

### 3.8 Racing and testing — ABSOLUTE
No cover for damage sustained during racing, time trials, speed testing,
track days, or driver-training events held on a closed circuit.

### 3.9 Flood and water — REBUTTABLE
No cover for damage caused by driving into floodwater where the water depth was
visible or signage prohibited entry.
**Rebuttal condition**: cover may be restored where the vehicle was stationary
and parked at the time of inundation, or where the driver entered the water in
response to a direction by emergency services.

### 3.10 Wear, tear and mechanical failure — ABSOLUTE
No cover for mechanical or electrical breakdown, wear and tear, rust,
corrosion, tyre wear, or gradual deterioration, unless such damage is a direct
consequence of an otherwise covered event.

### 3.11 Unsecured load and overloading — REBUTTABLE
No cover where the vehicle was carrying a load exceeding the manufacturer's
rated capacity or an unsecured load that contributed to the incident.
**Rebuttal condition**: cover may be restored where the overloading or
unsecured load is demonstrated not to have contributed to the incident.

### 3.12 Deliberate acts — ABSOLUTE
No cover for damage deliberately caused by the insured, a person acting with
the insured's consent, or a person resident with the insured.

### 3.13 Unattended vehicle with keys — REBUTTABLE
No theft cover where the vehicle was left unattended, unlocked, with keys or
keyless fob in or on the vehicle.
**Rebuttal condition**: cover may be restored where the vehicle was within a
locked private garage, or where the keys were taken by force or threat.

### 3.14 Unregistered or unroadworthy — ABSOLUTE
No cover where the vehicle was unregistered at the incident date, or where a
defect notice was in force and unremedied and that defect contributed to the
incident. Registration expired by 14 days or less is treated under clause 3.15.

### 3.15 Recently lapsed registration — REBUTTABLE
Where registration had expired by **14 days or less** at the incident date,
cover may be restored where registration was renewed within 14 days of the
incident and the vehicle was otherwise roadworthy.

### 3.16 Territorial limit — ABSOLUTE
Cover applies within Australia only. No cover applies to incidents occurring
outside Australian territory.

### 3.17 War, terrorism and nuclear — ABSOLUTE
No cover for loss arising from war, invasion, civil war, terrorism, or nuclear
reaction or contamination.

### 3.18 Consequential loss — ABSOLUTE
No cover for loss of income, loss of use, depreciation in market value after
repair, or other consequential losses, except hire car benefit under clause 4.2
where applicable.

## 4. Benefits and Sub-limits

### 4.1 Sum insured basis
Comprehensive policies are written on either **agreed value** or **market
value** basis as recorded on the policy schedule. Settlement follows
POL-STL-007 section 2.

### 4.2 Hire car benefit
Where the policy carries the hire car benefit HC-01, a replacement vehicle is
provided for up to **21 days** following a covered event, or until settlement
of a total loss, whichever is earlier. Daily rate capped at AUD 85.

### 4.3 Windscreen benefit
One windscreen or window glass claim per policy period is payable without
excess under comprehensive policies. A second and subsequent glass claim in the
same period attracts the standard excess.

### 4.4 Personal effects
Personal effects in the vehicle damaged or stolen during a covered event are
covered to **AUD 1,000** per claim. Mobile phones, laptops and tools of trade
are excluded from this benefit.

### 4.5 Emergency accommodation and travel
Where a covered event occurs more than 100 km from the insured's residence,
reasonable emergency accommodation and travel costs are covered to
**AUD 1,000** per claim.

### 4.6 Trailer and caravan
Trailers under 750 kg are covered for third-party property damage only.
Caravans and trailers over 750 kg require separate cover.

---

## 5. Third Party Liability

### 5.1 Property damage limit
Liability for damage to third-party property is covered to **AUD 20,000,000**
per event across all tiers.

### 5.2 Bodily injury
Bodily injury liability is covered by the relevant state Compulsory Third Party
scheme and is not covered under this product. Injury claims must be referred to
the CTP insurer and recorded against the claim.

### 5.3 Legal costs
Reasonable legal costs incurred with the insurer's prior written consent are
covered. Costs incurred without consent are not recoverable.

---

## 6. Edge Cases and Interaction Rules

### 6.1 Multiple exclusions
Where both an absolute and a rebuttable exclusion apply, the absolute exclusion
governs and the claim must be denied regardless of any rebuttal.

### 6.2 Exclusion applying to one component only
Where an exclusion applies only to part of the claimed loss, the remainder must
be assessed and settled under POL-CLM-001 clause 7.3.

### 6.3 Driver-specific exclusions and the policyholder
Exclusions in clauses 3.2 to 3.5 attach to the driver at the incident date, not
the policyholder. Where the policyholder was not the driver, the exclusion
still defeats the claim for own damage, but third-party property liability
under clause 5.1 remains payable.

### 6.4 Storm damage to a vehicle under repair
Where the insured vehicle is damaged by a covered event while in the custody of
a Meridian-approved repairer, the repairer's own insurance responds first.
Meridian responds only for the shortfall and no excess applies.

### 6.5 Two claims one incident
Where a single incident generates both a theft claim and a collision claim
(for example a stolen vehicle later crashed), the claims are handled as one
claim with one basic excess under POL-STL-007 clause 3.6.
