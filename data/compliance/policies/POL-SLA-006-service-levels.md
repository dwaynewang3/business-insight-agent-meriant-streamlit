# POL-SLA-006 — Service Levels and Turnaround Standards

## 1. Purpose and Relationship to Other Policies

### 1.1 Purpose
This policy sets how claim timeframes are measured, the decision deadlines that
apply to every claim, the portfolio service targets reported to management,
and the modifications that apply to catastrophe events and vulnerable
claimants.

### 1.2 Relationship to POL-CLM-001
Timeframes stated in POL-CLM-001 are mandatory and are not repeated as separate
obligations here. This policy:
- (a) consolidates all claim timeframes for reporting (section 4);
- (b) sets the clock rules used to measure them (section 2);
- (c) adds the decision deadlines in section 3; and
- (d) modifies specific POL-CLM-001 timeframes for catastrophe events
  (section 6) and vulnerable claimants (section 7). Where a clause in this
  policy modifies a POL-CLM-001 clause, it names the clause it modifies.

---

## 2. Clock Rules

### 2.1 Clock start
Unless a clause states otherwise, a timeframe expressed as "within N business
days of" an event is met if the action occurs on or before the Nth business day
after the day of the event. The day of the event is day 0.

### 2.2 Business days
Business days are as defined in POL-CLM-001 clause 1.3 (Monday to Friday,
excluding NSW public holidays). The NSW Bank Holiday is not a public holiday for
this purpose.

### 2.3 Suspension
A timeframe is suspended while:
- (a) the insurer is waiting for documents requested in writing under
  POL-DOC-005 clause 7.1, from the date of the request until receipt;
- (b) enhanced verification is in progress under POL-FRD-004 clause 4.2;
- (c) the claim is referred to the SIU, until the SIU outcome is issued; or
- (d) the claimant has asked in writing for the claim to be put on hold.

### 2.4 Recording suspensions
A suspension must be recorded in the claims system with a start date, end date
and reason code. An unrecorded suspension is disregarded when measuring
compliance.

### 2.5 Timeframes that cannot be suspended
Suspension does not apply to claim acknowledgement (POL-CLM-001 clause 6.1),
complaint timeframes (POL-CLM-001 clause 10.3), or the overall decision cap in
clause 3.3.

---

## 3. Decision Deadlines

### 3.1 Standard and complex claims
A decision to accept, partially accept or deny must be made within
**20 business days** of the docs-complete date.

### 3.2 Fast-track claims
A fast-track claim must be decided within **5 business days** of the
lodgement date.

### 3.3 Overall cap
Every claim must be decided within **four months** of the lodgement date. Where
a decision cannot be made within four months, the claimant must receive written
notice of the reasons and of their right to complain before the four-month
date.

### 3.4 Total loss settlement offers
A total loss settlement offer must be made within **10 business days** of the
docs-complete date.

## 5. Portfolio Service Targets

### 5.1 Targets
Targets are measured monthly per claim type and reported to the Chief Claims
Officer.

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Claims acknowledged within 2 business days | ≥ 98% |
| Fast-track claims decided within 5 business days | ≥ 90% |
| Standard and complex claims decided within 20 business days of docs-complete date | ≥ 85% |
| Accepted settlements paid within 10 business days | ≥ 95% |
| Average motor cycle time, lodgement to payment | ≤ 25 business days |
| Claims with a status-update breach | ≤ 2% |

### 5.2 Measurement rules
Recorded suspension periods are excluded from measured cycle times. Claims
linked to a declared catastrophe event are reported separately and excluded
from the monthly targets in clause 5.1.

### 5.3 Escalation
Where any target is missed for **two consecutive months**, the Head of Claims
Operations must report the cause to the Chief Claims Officer and table a
remediation plan within 20 business days.

---

## 6. Catastrophe Events

### 6.1 Extended decision deadlines
For claims linked to a declared catastrophe event, the decision deadlines in
clauses 3.1 and 3.2 are doubled.

### 6.2 Timeframes not extended
The following are not extended during a catastrophe: claim acknowledgement
(POL-CLM-001 clause 6.1), the overall decision cap (clause 3.3), and payment of
accepted settlements (POL-CLM-001 clause 8.1).

---

## 7. Vulnerable Claimants

### 7.1 Status updates for vulnerable claimants
Where a claimant is flagged as vulnerable, a status update must be given at
least every **10 business days** while the claim is open. This modifies
POL-CLM-001 clause 6.2 for vulnerable claimants.

### 7.2 Single point of contact
A vulnerable claimant must be offered a single named point of contact for the
life of the claim.

### 7.3 Flagging
A vulnerability flag must be recorded within 1 business day of the
vulnerability being identified, with the claimant's consent where practicable.

---

## 8. Breach Recording

### 8.1 Service breaches
Each missed timeframe must be recorded as a service breach with a reason code,
as required by POL-CLM-001 clause 10.1.

### 8.2 Claimant-caused delay
A delay caused solely by the claimant is not a service breach where the
suspension was recorded under clause 2.4.
